# Can wave exposure and a mixed-layer heat constraint improve GBR bleaching warnings?

**Study status, 2 November 2026.** Reefactor can build a common environmental cube and link real GBR observations to its past. It has yet to demonstrate improved biological predictions on independent events. The feasibility result with the greatest bearing on that test is spatial replication. At 0.25°, 5,111 central GBR colony rows share just **three environmental cells and ten cell-date histories**. A random split of those rows could reward a model for recognizing the same exposure twice.

## Environmental and biological windows

All experiments use the official Marine Park polygon, UTC daily environmental fields and 0.25° cells. The full planning grid extends from 24.5°S to 10°S and 142°E to 154.25°E. The earlier rectangle omitted the southern tip. The revised envelope covers the polygon, including the small sliver east of 154°E. Each retained cell has every selected field on every requested date. Coarse-cell centroids can still exclude observations on reefs near a coastline or boundary. Report this attrition by reef sector and shelf position. [Reef Authority spatial data](https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au/about-us/spatial-data-information-services)

There are **148 registered gridded fields** in total. The remaining six are four monthly global chemistry fields and two static context fields stored separately. Combining all source calendars also gives December 2010 to April 2019, but a daily checkpoint with all 148 inputs would need versioned monthly publication times, causal joins and a fresh support audit. An eReefs field ending in 2019 cannot be part of a complete 2026 input. The 142 daily fields include 33 diagnostics, 16 simulated responses and a DHW reference. Their inclusion in a fetch does not grant predictor eligibility.

The long series includes currents, near-surface temperature, salinity, sea level, mixed-layer depth, oxygen, primary production, chlorophyll, nitrate, phosphate and silicate. The wave release adds significant height, zero-crossing, energy and peak periods, and eastward/northward Stokes drift. Its live 202411 catalogue reaches **2026-08-31 21:00 UTC**. All eight three-hourly snapshots must be present for a native cell-day average. Directional angles and conditional swell partitions need separate circular statistics and support rules. ERA5 supplies wind, air temperature and dew point, pressure, cloud, radiation, heat and freshwater fluxes. ### Factors outside the long daily cube

The [driver inventory](../src/reef/data/bleaching_drivers.json) contains **190 entries in 16 families**. It separates drivers, susceptibility modifiers, responses, proxies and candidates with sparse evidence. The [source registry](../src/reef/catalog.py) gives each admitted field's native variable, units, source resolution and role. An exhaustive list of all possible causes is an open scientific question.

* **Light and physiology.** Underwater PAR, UV, spectral quality, attenuation and shading modify the light exposure experienced by symbionts. Laboratory work also supports studying nocturnal artificial light, tissue irradiance, skeletal backscatter, electron transport and non-photochemical quenching. Satellite night-light radiance lacks the depth, spectrum and water clarity needed to establish a colony's dose. Admit it only after local calibration. [Experimental night lighting](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0269749120305303) · [Microscale optical experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5258690/) · [NASA Black Marble](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/VNP46A2)
* **Water quality and habitat.** The rich window adds sediment, oxygen demand, dissolved carbon, alkalinity, carbonate saturation, nutrients, food supply and water optics. Queensland pesticide and catchment monitoring add measured exposures at stations. River concentrations, pesticide mixtures and sediment deposition need plume transport or measured reef exposure before spatial admission. Colony-depth temperature, tidal emersion, near-bed orbital velocity and night-time hypoxia are finer-scale processes than our daily surface cell. [Regional eReefs archive](https://thredds.ereefs.aims.gov.au/thredds/catalog/catalog.html) · [Queensland monitoring](https://www.reefplan.qld.gov.au/tracking-progress/paddock-to-reef/modelling-and-monitoring)
* **Host and site susceptibility.** The 2022 release adds actual colony area, taxon, exposure, depth zone, shelf position, plot rugosity, slope and fractal dimension, alongside modeled near-bed velocity. The One Tree Reef release tracks *Goniopora*, black band disease, recovery, partial mortality and reef-depth logger temperature across 2024–February 2025. Its public catalogue and four file digests were verified. Its anonymous API required a token and the website returned a browser-validation page, so those rows have not been imported. Disease, mortality and recovery have separate outcome definitions. [AIMS 2022 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14752226) · [One Tree Reef archive](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.37pvmcvxr)
* **Remaining gaps.** Genotype, symbiont community, microbial and viral states, energy reserves, contaminants and diver-scale hydrodynamics have incomplete coverage. Keep them in site or specimen studies with explicit sampling units. Reassess new releases by mechanism, primary evidence, units, temporal support, spatial support, independence and as-of availability. [Coral Trait Database](https://www.coraltraits.org/procedures) · [AIMS microbial partnerships](https://www.aims.gov.au/research-topics/marine-life/marine-microbes/microbial-partnerships)

## Publication test

**Primary question.** For an observed GBR hard-coral survey, do wave exposure and ocean state improve seven-day predictions of a chart-defined bleaching signal over a calibrated DHW baseline in later events and geographically held-out cells? A second, paired comparison asks whether imposing the mixed-layer heat budget on the SST head changes biological skill when the feature set, training budget and test surveys are identical.

A GBR study already tested 25 predictors across the 2016, 2017 and 2020 events. Reef-scale aerial severity has also been modeled across eight events through 2025. These are direct comparators. The possible contribution here is a reproducible, lead-time-limited, matched-support test of added physical information against actual later observations. More fields, a larger model or lower SST error alone cannot establish that claim. [Cheung et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.70105) · [Eight-event study methods](https://marine-ecologist.github.io/GBR-dhw/methods.html) · [AIMS 2025–26 condition report](https://www.aims.gov.au/monitoring-great-barrier-reef/gbr-condition-summary-2025-26)

1. **Freeze the cohort before fitting.** The executable design audit finds 4,674 dated, quality-eligible chart surveys and 4,557 after calendar and grid-centre checks. Train through 2021, tune in 2022–24, and reserve 2025 through August 2026. Rotate five deterministic partitions of whole 1° blocks. Each eligible later observation is tested once. Purge crossing temporal histories and exclude training cells within 50 km of validation or test cells. Generate a final row ledger with exclusions for source support, vintage, survey quality and duplicates. Restrict the encoder's spatial context and normalization to permitted training geography as well. The existing generic chronological trainer alone does not enforce this design.
2. **Check effective sample size.** Before full-cube availability filtering, the five test rotations contain 15, 2, 1,059, 59 and 3 surveys. Together they cover **1,138 surveys, 14 exposure cells and 10 blocks**. One validation rotation has only one survey. Freeze model choices in adequate development folds or use a common prespecified setting. Report years, cells, blocks, flags and non-flags for each fold after locking the design. A fold with one outcome class cannot estimate discrimination. A lack of independently exposed reefs warrants a scope-limited or negative result.
3. **Build the baselines first.** Compare training prevalence, a calibrated lead-safe DHW model and a regularized multivariable baseline using training-era normalization. Then compare recurrent models with thermal history alone, ocean fields, added waves, and full meteorology. Pair physics-on and physics-off runs at identical capacity and seed. Sweep 1k, 10k, 100k and approximately 1M weights on development data only. Predeclare a small seed set and a search budget. Restrict the eReefs ablation to matched dates, survey rows and cells in the shorter window.
4. **Measure the target of the claim.** Use paired Brier-score difference for the chart flag, macro-averaged by exposure block, with reliability and prevalence plots. Report PR-AUC with class counts and survey-weighted scores as secondary views. For percentage surveys report MAE in percentage points. For colony ordinals report ranked probability score by site. Recompute paired differences under event- and block-level resampling. With only two recent years, report per-year results and sensitivity to deleting each block alongside conditional intervals. Do not treat colonies sharing an exposure as independent replicates.
5. **Try to falsify the result.** Compare against shuffled wave histories within season/block, remove common hindcast sources by family, and test sensitivity to turbidity, depth and survey effort. Evaluate held-out SST with persistence and reef-depth loggers. Report heat-residual units and magnitudes before and after the physics penalty. An improved residual alongside poorer biological calibration argues against the proposed usefulness. Reanalysis issue vintages and coastal resolution limit operational interpretations.
6. **Release gates.** Verify the ERA5 retrieval and units, audit the southern 403 and compute GBR-wide retained support. Build the container and repeat a pilot when its engine is available. Export source hashes, code revision, fixed domain, dated split IDs, model settings, seeds, training-only statistics, aggregate predictions and uncertainty. Check redistribution terms for each observational source. Draft a support map, cohort flow table, baseline/ablation table and a failure analysis. Record any design amendment before opening held-out outcomes.
